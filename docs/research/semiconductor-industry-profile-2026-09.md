# Semiconductor Industry Profile — Research Notes (KSIC 264)

**Purpose**: research backing for `industry-profiles/KSIC264.yaml` (Wave E pilot, PR-reviewed 2026-09). Every claim in the profile traces to the sources below; figures that are forecasts are marked as such and deliberately presented as ranges where the sources disagree.

## References

- WSTS (World Semiconductor Trade Statistics) — recent news releases and forecast revisions: https://www.wsts.org/76/recent-news-release (accessed 2026-09-09). The 2026 projection was revised upward during the year; the research captured both the ~USD 975B figure and the later "beyond USD 1.5T" release — the spread is recorded rather than reconciled, because the profile's cycle note depends on the *direction* (strong up-phase, AI-concentrated), not the exact landing point.
- SIA (Semiconductor Industry Association) — monthly global billings: https://www.semiconductors.org (accessed 2026-09-09).
- CRS Report R48642, "U.S. Export Controls and China: Advanced Semiconductors" — https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48642 (accessed 2026-09-09). VEU program context.
- Federal Register, "Framework for Artificial Intelligence Diffusion" interim final rule (2025-01-15) — https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/15/2025-00636/framework-for-artificial-intelligence-diffusion (accessed 2026-09-09).
- BIS press release, rescission of the AI Diffusion Rule (2025-05-13) — https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens (accessed 2026-09-09).
- Holland & Knight, "US Strengthens Export Controls on Advanced Computing Items" (Dec 2024 rule package) — https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2024/12/us-strengthens-export-controls-on-advanced-computing-items (accessed 2026-09-09).
- TSMC official 2nm technology page (N2 GAA, volume production from Q4 2025) — https://www.tsmc.com/english/dedicatedFoundry/technology/logic/l_2nm (accessed 2026-09-09).
- AnySilicon, "Samsung Begins Mass Production of Advanced 2nm GAA Chips" and HBM4 announcements — https://anysilicon.com/news/samsung-begins-mass-production-of-advanced-2nm-gaa-chips-strengthening-its-foundry-leadership/ (accessed 2026-09-09).
- SemiWiki forum thread on TSMC 2nm capacity ramp (~100k wafers/month target for 2026) — https://semiwiki.com/forum/threads/tsmcs-2nm-chips-the-results-are-out.24329/ (accessed 2026-09-09; forum-sourced capacity figure, treat as indicative).
- Rapidus 2nm trial production (2025-07) and 2027 volume-production target — as covered in the PatSnap Eureka industry report: https://eureka.patsnap.com/insight/semiconductor-2025-the-2nm-race-hbm4-breakthroughs-and-materials-revolution-reshaping-global-supply-chains (accessed 2026-09-09).

  only, and marked as such wherever repeated.
