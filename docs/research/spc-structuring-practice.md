# Research Note — SPC-Based Acquisition Structure Standard Practices (Including Korea)

> 🌐 External reference material. Researched: 2026-09-05. For comparing industry practices behind S10 investment structure design (ADR-0045/0106).

## Standard Practices

1. **Risk isolation (ring-fencing)**: an SPC is a vessel that separates acquisition debt·liabilities from
   the sponsor/parent company (Investopedia, PKF)
2. **Tax efficiency**: debt push-down lets acquisition financing interest be expensed at the target entity
   level; a 100% subsidiary benefits from the consolidated tax return regime (Korea)
3. **Korean practice**: it is common for a PEF to establish a new entity (SPC) and acquire the business by
   way of business transfer (Chambers Acquisition Finance 2026); LBO director breach-of-duty liability risk
   (the line of case law since the Hi-Mart case) is an essential review item in structure design
4. **Caveats**: thin capitalization (thin-cap)/interest expense recognition limits, and changes in
   legislation taxing overseas SPC income — tax·legal confirmation is mandatory before finalizing the
   structure
