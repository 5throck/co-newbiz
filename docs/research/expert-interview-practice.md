# Research Note — Expert Interview Standard Practices (PE Deal DD)

> 🌐 External reference material. Researched: 2026-09-05

## Standard Practices

1. **Scoping first**: start from broad market trends·risks·strategic fit and narrow down to financial/
   operational specifics (Apex Leaders)
2. **Expert sourcing·compliance**: mind MNPI (material non-public information); verify compliance when
   using expert networks (CleverX)
3. **Leveraging customer interviews**: uncovering unmet needs, evaluating the target company's customer
   support and innovation (Axial)
4. **Bias mitigation**: avoid leading questions, triangulate across multiple experts, use structured
   interview guides, prevent anchoring through call ordering, and guard against deal-team confirmation
   bias — a common recommendation across multiple sources
