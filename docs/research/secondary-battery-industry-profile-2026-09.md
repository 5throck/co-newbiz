## Honesty notes

- The KSIC anchor is the 11th-revision **중분류 282** (일차전지 및 이차전지 제조업) —
  chosen over a 세분류 so the profile covers the lithium-ion ecosystem end to end.
  If a deal binds a narrower scope (e.g. 28209 기타 이차전지), note it on the
  opportunity rather than splitting the profile.
- Capacity-utilization and investment-adjustment statements (2024–2025) summarize
  Korean cell-makers' public disclosures and policy reporting; no single numeric
  utilization figure is asserted — deals needing one must source it at the time
  of analysis.
- Solid-state timelines are vendor TARGETS with recorded analyst caution that
  volume may slip post-2030 — the profile treats 2027 as a small-scale start,
  not a mass-production date.
