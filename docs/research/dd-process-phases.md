# Research Note — M&A Due Diligence Phases and Timeline Standard Practices

> 🌐 External reference material. Researched: 2026-09-05. Reference for comparing the phase structure of the S9 DD track (Brownfield/Greenfield) against industry standards.

## Standard Practices

1. **Standard 6-phase flow**: Pre-LOI screening (judging deal worthiness with limited information) →
   LOI execution·securing exclusivity → confirmatory DD (the most extensive phase — QoE·legal·operations·
   customer verification) → negotiating deal terms (converting DD findings into price adjustments·
   indemnity·escrow) → pre-closing final confirmation → closing (dealroom, Peony)
2. **The confirmatory phase is the heart of DD**: most findings emerge after the LOI, when the full data
   room opens during exclusivity — before this phase, information is limited and judgments can only be
   preliminary
3. **Standard duration**: typically 30–45 business days from LOI to closing (mid-market basis); complex
   deals can exceed 90 days
4. **Risk persists after closing**: working capital true-ups, contracts nearing expiration, and similar
   items remain sources of friction after closing — closing does not equal risk elimination

## co-newbiz mapping

- The Brownfield track (target company DD) corresponds to the confirmatory DD phase — the S9 gate's
  "all applied checklist items adjudicated + zero mandatory items unmet" embodies the same principle as the
  standard exit condition of the confirmatory phase (all workstreams verified).
- The Greenfield track (validating plan feasibility) has no target company, so it does not map 1:1 to this
  standard process — it validates S5–S8 outputs (our plan), with the key difference being verification of
  internal assumptions rather than external target verification.
- S10 investment structure design corresponds to the "negotiating deal terms" phase, and S11 IC review to
  "pre-closing final confirmation."
