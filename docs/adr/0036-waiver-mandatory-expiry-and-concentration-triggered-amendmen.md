# ADR-0036: Waiver mandatory expiry and concentration-triggered amendment

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-08-20
**Deciders**: compliance-officer

## Context

An indefinite waiver is a de facto, unrecorded repeal of the clause it exempts; without a concentration signal, a regulation that doesn't match reality just accumulates silent workarounds instead of getting amended.

## Decision

Every waiver requires an expiry date — no indefinite waivers. Repeated waivers against the same clause are read as evidence the clause needs amendment, feeding directly into governance KPI-driven amendment priority (`docs/governance-operations.md` §2/§4), not as a compliance failure pattern.

## Consequences

Waiver concentration data, not intuition, sets the next amendment cycle's priority order.
