# ADR-0208: WACC credit spread follows the subject's credit rating — evidence-first rating ladder, Korean grade yields, server-derived audit

- Status: Accepted (2026-11-26)
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Deciders: New Business Review Lead (PM); method review quant-methodologist; profile-contract review region-lead; data-model review data-architect; third-party terms legal-counsel
- Amends: ADR-0115 (decision 4, cost of debt follows the company's credit)
- Related: ADR-0116 Phase 4 (WACC reference suggestions), ADR-0199 (DART evidence cache), ADR-0207 (same suggestion mechanism), ADR-0061 (money units), design `docs/designs/2026-11-26-wacc-credit-rating-spread-design.md`

## Context

The `credit_spread` suggestion is fixed to ECOS "corporate bond 3y AA-" minus "KTB 3y", so Kd = Rf + spread equals the AA- yield for every company (live: Ke 10.28%, Kd 4.55%). For a company below AA- the cost of debt, and therefore WACC, is understated. The user decided that the system should determine the company's credit rating automatically to choose the spread grade, with humans still confirming.

## Decision

1. **Rating ladder.** The suggestion's rating comes from, in order: (1) a **disclosed** rating from DART; (2) a **synthetic** rating from interest coverage; (3) a **default** `BBB-` with a reason. The rung used is always stated.
2. **Disclosed rating.** From the DART business report section `신용평가에 관한 사항` (and, as a supplement, the debt-securities issuance table and OpenDART `bdRs.json` `cdrt_int`). Domestic agencies (한국기업평가, 한국신용평가, NICE신용평가) and corporate-bond ratings only; latest per agency, **lowest across agencies**; agency, instrument, rating date and `rcept_no` recorded. Older than 18 months: stale chip, excluded from apply-all. Guaranteed, secured, structured and foreign-scale ratings are not issuer ratings and are skipped. A parse that finds nothing is a recorded status, never "unrated".
3. **Synthetic rating.** Coverage = operating income / interest expense, mapped with Damodaran's free published coverage-to-rating table (rating column only; his USD spreads are never used), stored in `region-profiles/KR.yaml` `credit_rating_references` under the profile contract (schema 1.4, `source` / `verified_on` / `maintainer`, `_validate.ts` rules). Interest expense hierarchy: interest expense, then interest paid (operating plus financing), then finance costs (flagged as an overstating proxy). Skipped for financial-sector companies and when interest expense is zero or missing.
4. **Collector extension.** DART collection today holds 8 accounts and no interest expense, pre-tax income or borrowings. The collector gains id-keyed interest and pre-tax-income accounts and name-pattern borrowing rows (existing 8 accounts unchanged), with `md_account` seed rows and a one-time re-sync of already-synced companies. Pre-tax income also feeds the ADR-0207 tax-rate suggestion.
5. **Rating to spread.** Korean market sources only, in a recorded basis ladder: KOFIA grade yield (target primary, **not built until KOFIA's terms for automated collection are confirmed in writing, or a maintainer-transcribed monthly snapshot is adopted**) > ECOS AA- and BBB- anchors with **geometric interpolation by notch** for A+ to BBB (AAA to AA- clamp to AA-; BB+ and below clamp to BBB- with an understatement chip) > ECOS clamp. ECOS publishes only AA- and BBB- (verified), so the ECOS BBB- series `ecos:721Y001:7030000` is collected. Spread = grade yield - KTB 3y of the same source and date. The interpolated figures are labelled as derived, not published.
6. **Cross-check, not override.** Implied cost = interest expense / average interest-bearing debt, compared with the market-built Kd; warn beyond 2.0 percentage points (registry parameter). Recent bond issuance coupons and ratings from DART are shown as evidence. Nothing changes the number.
7. **Sources.** Free only (ADR-0207 D2). Excluded: paid evaluators (KIS P&I, NICE P&I, KAP); KRX data portal (login, copy restrictions, thin trading); agency websites (copyright and crawler policies: human verification aid only, home-page links). Deferred, each needing its own ADR amendment when onboarded: KOFIA grade yields; data.go.kr FSC bond basic-info and price APIs (KOGL type 2 / type 4, legal reading of "commercial use" required) - **both onboarded by the slice 4 amendment below (2026-11-27): KOFIA automated collection stays OFF by default until KOFIA confirms in writing; the FSC APIs are cross-check evidence only.** The status of every source fact is in the design section 4, with unverified items marked.
8. **Storage.** New additive table `dart_credit_rating_snapshot` (immutable rows per `rcept_no` and parser version; money in INTEGER KRW millions per ADR-0061), a separate collect route `.../wacc/credit-rating/collect` (async, analyst role, audited), cache-only reads in the suggestions GET. No change to `dart_business_report_extract`.
9. **Apply and audit.** Apply is client-side. Apply-all includes disclosed (not stale) and synthetic ratings and excludes default and stale-disclosed ones (listed as missing with a reason). The confirmation of `wacc.debt_spread` accepts `credit_rating_snapshot_id`, `credit_rating_basis`, `reference_as_of`; the **server re-derives** rating, source, agency, date, spread basis and series from stored evidence, derives the edited flag by comparing the confirmed spread (the client flag is ignored), and extends the `assumption_set` audit target. Unknown snapshot id: 400. Manual spreads audit as before.
10. **Human boundary.** The rating is a model input with provenance, not a credit opinion. Confirmation stays a user-confirmed component; no gate, RAG grade, kill-criteria or data-confidence effect.
11. **ADR triggers.** DB schema (new table), master-data code system (new `md_account` rows), external data source changes (ECOS series; DART section and `bdRs.json` as new evidence uses). Lifecycle: `region-profiles/**` rule; maintainer quant-methodologist; yearly re-verification.

## As-built (2026-11-26, slice 2: disclosed rating)

Decisions 2, 7, 8 and 9 are implemented for the disclosed rung; decisions 3, 4 and 6 (synthetic rating, collector extension, cross-check) and the KOFIA and data.go.kr sources remain pending in later slices. Details: design section 17.

- **Storage.** Migration `0200_dart_credit_rating_snapshot.sql` (additive, reversible, rows immutable by trigger, unique per `source_rcept_no` and `parser_version`). The status vocabulary adds `not_disclosed` and `parse_failed`; parser `cr-1`. Evidence from the business report and from OpenDART `bdRs.json` both feed the selection: fresh beats stale, the lower rating wins, and differing sources are both shown.
- **Collection.** `POST/GET /api/projects/[id]/wacc/credit-rating/collect`: analyst role, `202` with a `job_run`, `409 already_running` (one per project and per company), `503 dart_api_key_missing`, `400 no_subject_company` / `400 no_dart_corp_code`, audited `wacc_credit_rating.collect_requested`. The suggestions GET reads the cache only and never calls DART. It is a user action; it is not chained to the company financial sync.
- **Registry.** `credit_rating_references.agencies` in `region-profiles/KR.yaml`; an alias change is a maintainer attestation because the alias list decides what counts as a domestic agency.
- **Audit.** A disclosed `wacc.debt_spread` confirmation requires `credit_rating_snapshot_id` (unknown or another company's id: 400, no run); the server re-derives rating, agencies, filing number and rating date from stored snapshots and ignores the client edited flag. A disclosed rating whose spread fell back to the AA- legacy value is excluded from apply-all (`credit_spread_legacy_basis`).
- **Amendment 2026-11-26 (user-approved): credit-bureau issuer rating rung.** The ladder becomes corporate-bond rating, then credit-bureau issuer rating, then synthetic (slice 3), then the BBB- default. Many DART filings carry only a yearly `기업신용평가` table from credit-information bureaus (NICE디앤비, 이크레더블, ...), not a bond rating. Its latest row (the lowest grade when several bureaus rated on that date) is used as a LOWER-CONFIDENCE basis: `rating_source: bureau`, never counted as `disclosed`, excluded from apply-all (`credit_rating_bureau_basis`) but individually applicable, same 18-month stale rule, and used only when there is no fresh bond rating. The bureau's cash-flow grade (CR-3, C+, B) is kept in a separate field and never read as the rating. Confirmation accepts `credit_rating_basis: "bureau"` with `credit_rating_snapshot_id`; the server re-derives it like `disclosed`. Parser `cr-2`; registry agencies gain `kind: bureau` (aliases await maintainer attestation). No schema change: the rows live in the existing `ratings_json` with instrument `issuer_bureau`. Design section 17.3.

## As-built (2026-11-26, slice 3: collector extension, synthetic rung, cross-check)

Decisions 3, 4 and 6 are implemented; the KOFIA and data.go.kr sources remain deferred. Details: design section 17.4.

- **Master data and collector.** Migration `0201_md_account_interest_debt.sql` (additive, reversible; a master-data code-system change, covered by decision 11) seeds 11 `md_account` rows: six id-keyed accounts (pre-tax income, interest expense in two shapes, finance costs, and the two interest-paid rows that share one name in the filing) and five borrowing classes. `fetchFinancials` returns them as per-year `extraItems`; the eight original accounts, `items` and the "found" decision are unchanged. Borrowing rows are classified by name, balance sheet only, and summed per class. The fact_financial writer resolves the new accounts by `ifrs_code` and fails loudly on a missing row. A data step clears `financial_checked_at`; an incremental sync then refreshes only the latest year, so the manual company sync (five years) is the re-sync that fills the earlier years (runbook `docs/runbooks/beta-series-collection.md`).
- **Synthetic rung.** `db/lib/credit-synthetic.ts`: coverage = operating income / interest expense (hierarchy: interest expense, interest paid summed, finance costs flagged as a proxy), lower-inclusive bands from the Damodaran large non-financial table now stored in `region-profiles/KR.yaml` (`credit_rating_references.synthetic`, verified against the publisher's page on 2026-11-26, values transcribed unchanged; the table publishes 15 of the 20 notches). Financial-sector companies skip the rung; md_company has no industry code, so the sector is detected from the company name by a registry rule (`synthetic.financial_sector`), which is a heuristic and a maintainer attestation.
- **Ladder.** Fresh bond rating, fresh bureau rating, synthetic, a stale bond or bureau rating, then the default. A stale rating ranks below the estimate and stays visible as `rating_superseded`. **Amendment 2026-11-26 (user-approved, after a live check): the synthetic rating is NOT part of apply-all** (decision 9 amended for synthetic). It is listed in `apply_all_excluded` with `credit_rating_synthetic_basis` and in the missing list, and stays individually applicable and individually auditable; apply-all still includes only fresh disclosed ratings. A loss-making company is flagged by a chip.
- **Cross-check.** `crosscheck` on the credit entry: implied cost vs the market Kd, `warn` when the gap exceeds `warn_gap_pp` in EITHER direction (the boundary is not a warning; an implied cost below Rf is judged by the gap like any other, amendment 2026-11-26 after a live check showed a 3.64% implied cost against a 10.36% market Kd), with reason code `crosscheck_implied_far_below_market` when the implied cost is more than `warn_gap_pp` below the market Kd; `unreliable` only for unusable inputs (a non-positive debt balance); `unavailable` with a recorded reason. `issuance_evidence` lists recent bond coupons. Neither changes a number.
- **Audit.** `credit_rating_basis: "synthetic"` is re-derived by the server (coverage, interest source, fiscal year recorded in the rationale); a relabelled basis is a 400 in both directions, a snapshot id is not allowed with it.
- **Tax rate.** Pre-tax income is now collected, so the ADR-0207 tax-bracket suggestion prefers it for every company synced after the migration (no code change; covered by test).
- **UI.** Source chip "interest-coverage estimate", an evidence block (coverage, period, interest source, estimate caveats, superseded rating), the cross-check line with a warning chip, issuance coupons, and a financial re-sync row in the reference-data collection block.

## As-built (2026-11-27, slice 4: KOFIA grade yields behind a switch, FSC bond cross-check)

Decision 7's two deferred sources are onboarded, each with the limits below (user decisions 2026-11-27). Details: design section 17.5.

- **KOFIA grade yields (decision 5, first rung of the basis ladder) - automated collection OFF by default.**
  - *Source.* The public page 시가평가 > 채권시가평가수익률 > 일자별 of `kofiabond.or.kr` (no login) gets its data from one undocumented XML service of the site itself (`POST /proframeWeb/XMLSERVICES/`, app `BIS-KOFIABOND`, service `BISBndSrtPrcSrchSO`, functions `getExistMaxDate`, `getHeadList`, `selectDay`). The calls were read from the page's own client file and probed once each on 2026-11-27: they answered with the valuation-firm average (평가사 평균) yields of the 회사채 I (공모사채) 무보증 AAA to BBB- rows and the KTB 3y row. This is a different, richer service than the community-gist one of design section 4.1 (`BISLastAskPrcROPSrchSO`, AA- and BBB- only), which was not used.
  - *The gate.* KOFIA has not confirmed that automated collection is permitted (no clause found, `robots.txt` absent: the path answers a 302 to an error page, Q&A of 2022-08-05 silent on automation). The collector therefore runs **only when env `CO_NEWBIZ_KOFIA_COLLECT` is exactly `on`; the default and every other value is off**, until KOFIA confirms **in writing** that automated collection of these yields is allowed; that confirmation is to be filed with this ADR before the switch is turned on. Off: the collector refuses before any network call, the collect route answers `503 kofia_collect_disabled` (no run, no audit row), the WACC panel shows no KOFIA collect row. Stored observations are read regardless of the switch (the gate is on fetching). The service is undocumented, so a layout change fails the run loudly and writes nothing; it is **unverified** that the service stays stable.
  - *Storage.* No new table and no migration: day-grain observations in `fact_market_observation` (series `kofia:corp-public-unsecured:<GRADE>:3y` for the ten grades and `kofia:ktb:3y`, source `kofia`, unit percent), the KRX day-grain pattern. The KTB 3y of the same answer is stored with them, so spread = grade yield - KTB 3y always comes from one source and one date. Source URL, series and date are carried on the suggestion (`kofia`).
  - *Precedence.* Basis `kofia_grade` (spread = grade 3y yield - same-date KOFIA KTB 3y, no interpolation, not derived; flat notches KOFIA writes as AA0, A0, BBB0 map to AA, A, BBB) outranks `ecos_interpolated` and `ecos_clamped` whenever the rating is one of AAA..BBB- and stored KOFIA data exists for the as-of month, where the as-of month is the later of the ECOS month and the KOFIA month (a newer ECOS month wins; BB+ and below use ECOS). The suggestion's Rf stays the ECOS risk-free entry. The confirmation re-derives at the cited `reference_as_of` month and records `spread_basis=kofia_grade`, `kofia_date` and `kofia_series` from stored data.
  - *UI.* Chip "grade yield (KOFIA)" (ko: 등급별 수익률(금융투자협회)), an evidence block (grade yield, same-date KTB, date, source link), and a "grade yields" row in the reference-data collection block that exists only when the switch is on.
  - *Scheduled collection (amendment 2026-11-27).* The data path is a scheduler job, not a click: `collect.kofia` (OS-level scheduler, ADR-0019/ADR-0189), weekday 18:45 KST by default (editable on the schedule page), seeded disabled, gated by the SAME switch (off: the run ends `skipped` with `kofia_collect_off`, no network call). It is a dedicated handler; the collector stays **out of `SOURCE_ADAPTERS`**, so the generic `fetch` / `md:sync` / `collect.*` paths still cannot reach KOFIA. Each run reads the service's latest published day (`getExistMaxDate`), stops when that day is stored, else stores it and backfills the business days still missing after the latest stored day within 10 calendar days (`selectDay` takes the date in `standardDt`; a holiday answers no rows and is skipped; one day is stored in one transaction). **Suggestions only read stored observations** (no code path from the suggestions route to the collector or the network; pinned by test). The manual button is a read-mostly fallback: while the stored data is up to date (latest stored day reaches the most recent weekday whose 18:30 KST publication has passed, or a successful check after it saw nothing newer) it answers `200 up_to_date` without a network call; a stale store is collected by any analyst; there is no force flag on the project route (admins cannot reach project routes, FR-SEC-03) - an admin forces a re-collect with run-now of `collect.kofia` (a manual run fetches the latest day again). The WACC panel row shows the latest stored day and the next scheduled run. Fixture-tested only: `selectDay` with a date other than the latest was not probed against the live service, and KOFIA's publication time is an assumption (a later publication is picked up by the next run's backfill).
- **FSC bond APIs on data.go.kr (decision 7): cross-check only.** `금융위원회_채권기본정보` (`GetBondIssuInfoService_V2/getBondBasiInfo_V2`) and `금융위원회_채권시세정보` (`GetBondSecuritiesInfoService_V2/getBondPriceInfo_V2`), both approved on the development account and verified live on 2026-11-27 with the repo key (the key in `.env` is the Encoding form and must be sent once-escaped: escaping it again reproduces `SERVICE_KEY_IS_NOT_REGISTERED_ERROR` even on the customs dataset approved in 2026-09, which is consistent with the failed 2026-11-26 probe, whose cause was not established). `GET /api/projects/[id]/wacc/bond-evidence` returns the issuer's outstanding bonds (coupon, maturity, balance in INTEGER KRW millions) and the latest price-implied yield of the five largest, as **reference evidence next to the credit spread: never an applied value, never in apply-all, never stored, never an audit input, no effect on any number**. A user-triggered live lookup (the suggestions GET never calls the portal), cached in process for 15 minutes. A portal problem (key not yet propagated, quota, expiry) is HTTP 200 `unavailable` with a reason code; a missing key is `503 data_go_kr_api_key_missing`. The issuer is matched by **name** (`md_company` has no corporate registration number: its `biz_reg_no` is the 10-digit business registration number, a different identifier, and a crno-only query is unreliable); several crno under one name = ambiguous, nothing shown.
  - *Terms (공공누리).* Basic info is KOGL type 2 (source attribution, no commercial use), price info is KOGL type 4 (source attribution, no commercial use, **no modification**): the panel carries the notice with dataset links, the price yield is shown exactly as published with its trading day, nothing is stored or exported. Whether internal valuation work counts as "commercial use" remains a legal-counsel reading and is **not** settled by this ADR.
- **Registry.** `docs/runbooks/data-go-kr-datasets.yaml`: both datasets `in_use` with endpoints, verified response fields and `used_by`; `tests/unit/data-go-kr-registry.test.ts` extended.
- **Verified live on 2026-11-27:** the three KOFIA service calls (once each, no login); both FSC operations (basic info for one issuer, price info for one ISIN, error shape of an unregistered key). **Fixture-only:** every parser, the precedence, the audit re-derivation and the UI rendering (fixtures are trimmed copies of those live answers; no live call in the test suite). **Not verified:** KOFIA's permission and the stability of its internal service; issuers with thousands of bonds (at most three pages of 100 are read); order guarantees of the price API (none assumed).

## Consequences

- Kd reflects the subject's credit instead of the AA- yield; the rating basis behind every confirmed spread is traceable and recomputable by the server.
- A new parsing dependency on free-text DART filings. Format drift is the main risk and is contained by recorded statuses (a failed parse never reads as "unrated"), fixtures and a visible source filing number.
- Interpolated A-range spreads are not published figures; they are disclosed as such. They depend on a thin BBB- ECOS series (a Kd above Ke is possible in the current market) until KOFIA grade yields are adopted.
- Standing maintenance of one more registry section; a collector and migration change with a one-time re-sync.
- The A-range of the ladder is only as good as the interpolation until a direct grade-yield source clears its terms: the KOFIA collector exists but is switched OFF by default (written confirmation pending), so until then, and for ratings KOFIA does not publish, the ECOS bases apply.
- One more undocumented-service dependency (KOFIA) that fails loudly on a layout change, and one more portal quota/approval dependency (data.go.kr) that degrades to an 'unavailable' note.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep AA- for everyone: rejected, understates Kd and WACC for non-AA- companies.
- Two-bucket step rule (A and above to AA-, below to BBB-): rejected as the primary rule (a 5.8-point cliff between A- and BBB+ at current ECOS levels); kept as the `ecos_clamped` fallback basis.
- Scrape KOFIA, KRX or agency sites now: rejected, terms unconfirmed or restrictive; an undocumented KOFIA endpoint is not verified to work.
- Use data.go.kr bond APIs in the first slice: rejected, key not activated, field names unverified, KOGL restrictions; DART already provides coupon and rating per issue.
- Rating from the market-implied Kd of listed bonds only: rejected, thin trading and no rating in the FSC basic-info API.
- Reuse `dart_business_report_extract` for credit data: rejected, couples to the ADR-0199 brownfield-evaluation windows and its extractor-version re-fetch.
- Default AA- or a missing suggestion when unrated: rejected, flatters an unevidenced company or leaves the number to an uncited guess; BBB- default is excluded from apply-all so it never carries silently.
- Paid evaluators (KIS P&I, NICE P&I, KAP): rejected, free-only principle.
