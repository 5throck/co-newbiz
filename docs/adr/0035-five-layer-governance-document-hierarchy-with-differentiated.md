# ADR-0035: Five-layer governance document hierarchy with differentiated enforceability

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-08-20
**Deciders**: process-governance-manager

## Context

Treating every governance artifact as equally mandatory drives the field to route around it formally; treating everything as equally recommended means nothing gets followed reliably.

## Decision

Fix five layers — policy and procedure are mandatory (waiver required to deviate); guideline is recommended (a stated reason suffices); checklist is mixed at the item level (`mandatory: true|false`); template is recommended. Each layer has its own amendment authority and approver.
