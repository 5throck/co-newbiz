# Process Map — co-newbiz New Business Review

**Owner**: process-governance-manager
**Design of record**: [`docs/designs/2026-08-19-co-newbiz-design_ko.md`](https://github.com/5throck/ai-workspace-standards/blob/main/docs/designs/2026-08-19-co-newbiz-design_ko.md) section 6, refined by [Stage 1 meetings](../../memory/archive/meeting-2026-08-20-m1-process-skeleton.md) M1/M2/M6.

> **Do not confuse this with the product build Phase 0–6** (design section 4/10). This document maps the *business review process* the team's system implements. The product-build phases are how the team builds and ships that system; this map is what the system, once built, actually runs.

## Stage Gate Sequence

```
Screening → Pre-FS → Target Screening (brownfield) → FS → DD → Entity IC → Group IC
```

**Stage chain (flow.spec v2.13.0, ADR-0106)**: S1 market analysis → S2 opportunity discovery → S3 synergy/fit → S4 entry-mode decision → S5 capability assessment & entry strategy (GF) / S6–S7 target identification & screening (BF) → S8 business-case analysis → S9 DD & verification → **S10 deal structuring** → S11 investment review & execution → S12 post-investment management. (Korean stage names in the ko counterpart.) Stages are work intervals; gates (Screening~Group IC, unchanged ids) sit between them. Stage ≠ Gate; historical ADRs keep their pre-v2.2.0 numbering (ADR-0106 is the mapping of record).

**Agent-assignment authority**: `process/flow.spec.yaml` `activities[].agent` is authoritative for **stage-level pipeline dispatch** (who PM dispatches for the stage's work); each procedure's `agent_key` is authoritative for its **internal steps**. A divergence between the two for the same work is a defect to reconcile, not a choice (see AGENTS.md "Process authority layering"). Harness dispatch mechanics are the `deal-pipeline-orchestration` skill.

**Deal structuring stage (S10, ADR-0045/0106)**: between the DD gate and Entity IC, the deal structure is designed in `procedures/_shared/deal-structuring/` — the vehicle (direct / SPC / holdco / internal-venture spinoff / JV) plus the four mandatory axes (tax structure, liability isolation, financing leverage, exit structure) land in `deal_structure_hypothesis` with an artifact-referenced four-axis review, and the financing plan (`financing_sources_uses`, migration 0075) must balance (sources == uses > 0). The S11 IC package embeds this structure so the committees approve what S10 produced. The pre-FS step-2 hypothesis remains the early draft this stage matures. **Track-aware steps (deal-structuring v1.1.0, ADR-0160 Accepted)**: step 1 (asset/stock, stake, instrument mix — brownfield-agent) applies when `project.case_type = brownfield`; greenfield uses steps 4-7 instead — establishment vehicle (greenfield-agent; ef-01/ef-03 link when an entity is created), funding sources incl. project finance, government funds and subsidies, and lease versus build (financing-agent), phased capex tranches and release conditions (greenfield-agent with valuation-agent; rbd-15 for R&BD), incentives with clawback conditions (incentive-agent). The four-axis review and sources == uses > 0 stay shared. The flow.spec S10 activity list is unchanged in this revision: activity-level `applicableTracks` is not supported by the flow.spec schema (see ADR-0160 follow-ups).

**Runtime execution surfaces (ADR-0183)**: runtime LLM calls on the stage surfaces pass the mandatory PM gateway (activity catalog, all-platform parity), and the `/flows` console (`app/web-next/app/flows`) visualizes the stage chain above and offers a dry-run lane. Scope and boundaries: ADR-0183.

## Green Field vs. Brown Field Divergence

| | Green Field | Brown Field |
|---|---|---|
| Primary risk axis | Infrastructure, permits, talent, community | Deal structure, contingent liabilities, six-validator completeness recorded as 6 declared / N applicable / M completed (classification-aware, ADR-0048) |
| Screening-stage hard filter | Infrastructure minimums (wastewater before power) | Preliminary antitrust screen (rough HHI) |
| Unique mandatory validators | site-infrastructure-assessment, talent-pipeline-planning, community-partnership-planning | antitrust, hr-comp (executive CoC compensation), customer (CoC retention), incentive clawback, contingent-liability, certification-transferability — the steps 6-11 six (ADR-0048 registry decides which apply per classification) |
| Site/target comparison | Two-stage: hard pre-filter → weighted score (infra 30 / incentive-NPV 25 / logistics 15 / labor 15 / permit-critical-path 15%) | Deal structure hypothesis → valuation with sequenced special-topic linkage |

## Certification & Permit Tracks (Parallel, Not Merged)

Permits (`permit`/`permit_edge`) and certifications (`certification`/`certification_edge`) run as **separate DAGs with separate critical paths** — a permit lets you operate, a certification lets you sell. See the glossary entry *seon-geup (type approval)* in `glossary/industry.yaml` and design section 8.8-1.

## RACI Legend
R = Responsible (does the work), A = Accountable (owns the outcome, single name), C = Consulted (two-way input before decision), I = Informed (told after decision).

**Greenfield S9 workspace scope overlay (ADR-0160 addendum 2026-10-12 b, Accepted)**: a greenfield S9 is a verification of our own plan, not diligence of another company. `checklists/_overlays/greenfield-workspace-scope.yaml` (`ovl-greenfield-workspace-scope` v1.1.0, decision record DEC-20261012-01, owner process-governance-manager) classifies each of the 77 items of the seven ADR-0148 workspace checklists per greenfield sub-track as apply as-is (A), apply reinterpreted with a greenfield reading (R), replace with a pointer (X), not applicable with a user-facing reason (N), or conditional on the human-confirmed `site_required` (C). Counts (after the risk-officer review 2026-10-12; ADR-0160 accepted by the human decider 2026-10-13) — null sub-track A14/R42/X2/N19/C0 (56 judgeable); `existing_business_rbd` A13/R47/X1/N13/C3 (60 judgeable, 63 with the site in scope); `intra_venture` A11/R44/X4/N15/C3 (55 +3). For a greenfield project in this scope the hr tab is counted in S9 completion even when the project was created before 2026-10-01, and step 6 (core-team readiness) reads the hr item hdd-01. Checklist text is not edited and brownfield is unchanged. The overlay also maps each `greenfield-dd` step to the tab and items it is judged in (`steps` key) and carries interim greenfield tab labels. Rows without an approved human approval record, and items missing from the overlay, fail closed to A; the only binding item (fin-09) is A in every sub-track and the validator rejects any non-A code on a binding item; `checklists/_validate.ts` recomputes the counts and rejects mismatches. Overlay changes follow the `policies/**`/`procedures/**` lifecycle row (impact analysis, policy pin, ADR addendum). The overlay is embedded in the library projection (`scripts/co-newbiz/sync-library-projection.ts`) and read at runtime (`app/web-next/lib/library.ts` `getWorkspaceOverlay`, consumed by `app/web-next/lib/greenfield-subtrack.ts`), fail-closed to A on any missing overlay, item or unapproved row.

**Brownfield S9 workspace scope overlay (ADR-0162, Accepted)**: `checklists/_overlays/brownfield-workspace-scope.yaml` (`ovl-brownfield-workspace-scope` v1.1.0, decision record DEC-20261012-01, owner process-governance-manager) uses the same overlay schema to scope the 77 workspace items per brownfield sub-track by deal shape, with codes A/R/X/N only (C is rejected on brownfield). `m_and_a` is the baseline: every row is A and its extras are unscoped. Judgeable workspace items — `m_and_a` 77, `jv` 75, `strategic_investment` 71, `minority_investment` 71, `partnership` 52 (fdd-01/fdd-07 replaced by pt-03), `rbd` 77, `corporate_venture` 71. An `extra_checklists` block scopes the attached extras by section without changing which extras attach: deal-shape-presigning keeps jvd for `jv`, min for `minority_investment`, lic and cdv for `partnership`; pt-06 is N outside `jv`. Workspace plus extras gives 102 / 93 / 71 / 87 / 78 / 92 / 71 (revised after the 2026-10-12 legal-counsel review). Binding items (fin-09; bf-01..bf-03) can never be N, X or excluded, and fin-09 is A in every column (risk-officer review 2026-10-12; judgeable counts unchanged). `checklists/_validate.ts` dispatches on `track`, enforces the baseline, binding guard, attached X targets, reasons, readings and approvals, and recomputes all counts. Every row records the user's chat approval of the design (2026-10-12); the cross review in design section 11 is still pending. The brownfield overlay loader is wired at runtime: the overlay is embedded in the library projection (`scripts/co-newbiz/sync-library-projection.ts`) and read via `app/web-next/lib/library.ts` `getBrownfieldWorkspaceOverlay`, consumed by `app/web-next/lib/brownfield-subtrack.ts`, fail-closed to A on any missing overlay, item or unapproved row.

**S9 dispatch covers all seven DD domains (flow.spec v2.7.0, ADR-0160 addendum c, Accepted)**: alongside `commercial-dd`, `technical-dd`, `legal-dd` and `dd-waiver-review`, S9 declares `financial-dd` (financial-analyst), `hr-dd` (hr-integration-agent), `market-dd` (market-intelligence-agent) and `financing-dd` (financing-agent), each over `[greenfield-dd, brownfield-dd]` for both tracks. Each activity lists only skills owned by its own agent (`financing-dd` has none, since `financing-plan-review` belongs to valuation-agent). These are stage-level dispatch entries only; the procedures' `agent_key` values still own their internal steps, and the gate and completion criteria are unchanged.

The Entity IC and Group IC decision models both carry a fourth state, `withdrawn_pending_resubmission`, added per `docs/pilots/pilot-003-doosan-bobcat-robotics.md` finding F-2 — a real case's withdraw-and-re-propose pattern that the original three-state (approved/rejected/conditional) model couldn't represent.

**Parallel entry chain — deal-shape selection (ADR-0052 §A)**: `procedures/_shared/deal-shape-chain/` is a SECOND entry route alongside the market-shaped chains above, for capability-gap closures that want a partner before (or instead of) an acquisition: P0 need & funnel → P1 partnership screen → P2 partner DD → P3 deal-shape selection → P4 contracting (shape-keyed pre-signing gate, `checklists/deal-shape-presigning-checklist.yaml`) → P5 post-launch review. The full-M&A outcome routes OUT to the brownfield chain unchanged; the P3 decision *writes* the classification axes (ADR-0047 §A.5), which is why the chain itself carries no `applies_when` — it runs before classification exists.

**Post-close chain — PMI (ADR-0052 §B)**: where a Brown Field acquisition completes, `procedures/_shared/pmi-integration-execution/` owns signing → close → integration → BAU at the NEW `post_close` gate slot after Group IC: pre-close (IMO, integration thesis, clean team, staged synergy targets) → day-1 (continuity, control handover) → first 100 days (cadence, promised-vs-actual synergy, 30/60/90 attrition) → BAU exit — a human Finance certification (`bau-exit-certifier`); an uncertified synergy run-rate halts the handover. Non-control acquisitions run the reduced phase set (pre-close + day-1 + monitoring only), scoped by ADR-0045's stake axis.

**Post-formation lifecycle — JV governance (ADR-0052 §C)**: `procedures/_shared/jv-lifecycle-governance/` (`applies_when: origin = joint`) owns the decade between formation and exit that `entity-establishment-execution` deliberately stops at (its step-5 completion confirm, JV addendum ef-13..17): operate (board-approved governance framework, reserved-matters map, three-type CoI protocol, arm's-length pricing) → monitor (seven-dimension health scorecard; annual strategic asset review carrying the parent-side disciplines — designated executive accountability, portfolio gameboard, lookbacks) → restructure (metric-tied triggers, six-item menu, time-boxed deadlock ladder) → exit (the five-step practice exercising the formation-time ef-16 exit clauses — designed at formation, executed here). Steps 1–2 run on standing cadence, 3 annually, 4–5 on trigger — phases, not one-shot gates. Scorecard storage (a schema addition) is ADR-0052 open question (e), the prerequisite for the Section E portfolio script.

**FS quant dispatches + R&BD gating (ADR-0052 §D)**: both FS procedures now dispatch `quant-methodologist` — `monte-carlo-simulation` at valuation (seeded P50/P95 NPV + value-at-risk, `distributional_valuation`; proxy-chain reliability enters as demand-driver dispersion) and `scenario-planning` at risk assessment (downside/base/upside + the rate-shock covenant test, `scenario_risk_assessment`), closing the agent's zero-dispatch status on both tracks. The R&BD track's per-gate TRL/MRL evidence matrix (cutoffs ILLUSTRATIVE until the thresholds meeting), the GAO-20-48G evidence-quality rules (named tests/environments/artifacts, non-champion reviewer), and staged-funding-as-real-options discipline live in `checklists/rbd-technology-checklist.yaml` v1.3.0 (rbd-08..rbd-15) — enforced at pre-FS/DD via the referencing procedures, at the other gates via the `gate_pass_record` completeness check; gate vocabulary for tech bets adds Hold/Recycle alongside Go/Kill.

**Portfolio monitoring — venture cells (ADR-0052 §E)**: `procedures/_shared/portfolio-venture-monitoring/` (group level, `applies_when: origin in [internal, hybrid, joint]`) gives the venture-character cells (5/6/7/8/9/10) post-IC visibility: a bifurcated financial/strategic scorecard rollup per review cycle, a stage-transition evidence pack (tranche preconditions, contribution-responsibility obligations, time-boxed windows, six-option exit-path evaluation: integrate / standalone / spinout / reduce ownership / divest / kill), and a human tranche + kill/graduate decision (`venture-portfolio-ic` — a kill is a governed decision with learning capture, not a silent starve). The scripted view (stage-progression ledger, capital-vs-milestone comparison) waits on the scorecard-storage schema — ADR-0052 open question (e), the longest-lead chain.

**S11/S12 execution and post-management by track (flow.spec v2.10.1, ADR-0161, Accepted; Q1-Q7 decided by the user in chat 2026-10-12)**: greenfield and brownfield are told apart by `track`/`case_type`, never `origin`.
- **S11 greenfield execution baseline**: new procedure `procedures/_shared/greenfield-execution-baseline/` (applies when `project.case_type = greenfield`, gate slot Entity IC, owner greenfield-agent) and S11 activity `greenfield-execution-baseline`. Its six steps are: (1) capex tranche schedule bound to the live `capex_tranche` rows (migration 0136) and the S10 conditions, read and reconciled only, never writing tranche status, with gaps reported as findings; (2) permit and certification milestones, kept separate; (3) aggregate hiring plan; (4) ramp-up KPI targets against the S8 adopted case, with the ADR-0159 goals as reference only; (5) incentive obligation register; (6) human confirmation by one person with a mandatory rationale (`execution-baseline-confirmer`). When a new entity is formed, `entity-establishment-execution` runs alongside, unchanged. Checklist evidence is `entity-formation-checklist` gx-01..gx-03. At this S11 use, `financing-plan-checklist` fin-04..fin-08 are scoped to brownfield. The approved S9 overlay classification of those items is unchanged.
- **investment-structuring (Q6)**: the activity keeps one id and owner brownfield-agent. The greenfield owner is encoded at the procedure layer: `entity-ic-review` v1.3.0 step 3 (IC memo) applies to brownfield, and step 3.1 is the greenfield IC memo by greenfield-agent with financing-agent co-review.
- **S12 coverage**: `portfolio-venture-monitoring` v1.2.0 applies when `origin in [internal, hybrid, joint]` OR `case_type = greenfield`, so greenfield rows with a null origin are covered. It documents the greenfield ramp-up measure keys and a greenfield reading of the unchanged five-term transition vocabulary. Default `venture_class` is driving / enabling / emergent by sub-track, and the person recording can change it. External brownfield deals get `integration-monitoring` (`pmi-integration-execution` v1.1.0 step 6, post-close monitoring, m_and_a and strategic_investment; steps 1-5 unchanged) and `partnership-monitoring` (`jv-lifecycle-governance` v1.1.0 step 2, partnership variant; steps 1, 3-5 stay joint-origin only).
- **Not supported**: activity-level `applicableTracks`. Scoping is enforced by procedure `applies_when` plus the UI/API guards of design row 2. The S11 objective and the `requiredArtifacts` wording are unchanged in v2.8.0, because the app engine mirrors them verbatim.
- **Retroactive application (Q2)**: applied to in-flight projects by predicate, with no re-pin. A human records one `policy_migration` audit entry per project. New steps are recommended, not blocking, for stages already completed. Nothing is deleted and no gate is reopened.
- **Record**: decision record DEC-20261012-02.
