## Classification per sub-track

| Item | strategic_investment | minority_investment | corporate_venture |
|---|---|---|---|
| ma-01 Relevant market, HHI / HHI-delta | R | R | R |
| ma-02 Multi-jurisdiction merger-control filing determination | A | A | A |
| ma-03 Gun-jumping | R | R | R |
| min-05 Foreign-investment screening | A | A (already applied) | R |

Rationale:

- **ma-02 is the trigger test**, so it is A wherever a filing can plausibly be triggered by a stake acquisition without control (strategic and minority stakes: several regimes count share or voting thresholds, or material/competitively significant influence, not only control). For CVC it is R: tickets are usually small and below thresholds, but the determination and its basis must still be recorded, and passive-investment exemptions typically fall away when board or observer rights are taken.
- **ma-01** (market definition, HHI) is R everywhere: required when ma-02 finds a filing obligation or when there is a horizontal or vertical overlap, including common-ownership effects where the investor already holds a competitor stake (pilot 010 F-2); otherwise the "no overlap, no filing" basis is recorded.
- **ma-03** (gun-jumping) is R everywhere: it bites only where a filing obligation exists; the reading names the minority-stake-specific risks (exercising board nomination or veto rights, exchanging competitively sensitive information before clearance).
- **min-05** (FDI screening) is A for strategic and minority stakes because the item text is already conditional on stake size and nationality; R for CVC, where the reading flags non-controlling-investment triggers (sensitive technology, defence, critical infrastructure, board/observer rights, access to non-public technical information).
