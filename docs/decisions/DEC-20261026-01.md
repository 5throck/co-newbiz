- **Corrected premise.** DEC-20261025-06 ruling 2 adopted the co-export 9-section skeleton (Legal Basis first) for all 39 rewritten agents on the premise that sibling co-* projects share it. Verified 2026-10-26: only co-export and co-newbiz use `## Legal Basis`; other siblings use different skeletons.
- **Criterion.** KEEP when the agent's judgments rest on concrete statutes, regulations, or permit/certification regimes (the section cites such a regime or the agent's work is applying one). REMOVE when the section is ADR / flow.spec / procedure / checklist authority prose, internal-rule governance, or technical, analytical or operational method, including agents that touch regulated topics whose statutory judgment is owned by a KEEP sibling.
