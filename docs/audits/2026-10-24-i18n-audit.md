## 3. Glossary adherence (skill step 3, YAML adaptation)

Matching rules (fixed by this audit and encoded in `tests/unit/i18n-audit.test.ts`):

- A term **applies** to a key when the ko master value contains the term's ko form as a whole word — no adjacent same-script character (Hangul terms get Hangul boundaries, Latin/digit terms get `[A-Za-z0-9_]` boundaries). `확정` inside `확정일자` is not a match.
- The en counterpart **adheres** when it contains, case-insensitively and as a whole word (optional plural `-s`), the term's en rendering **or the term name** (acronym terms: the UI legitimately says "DSCR", not the full "Debt Service Coverage Ratio" — the glossary schema itself defines the `en` field as the term used in code/docs), or the common-noun forms "greenfield"/"brownfield" for Green Field / Brown Field (T5 review note).

**Reading**: the drift is dominated by terminological granularity, not mistranslation. The two largest classes are one-word ko terms whose glossary en rendering is a multi-word status label — `확정` ("Confirmed Status") is also the ordinary verb "confirm", and `게이트` ("Stage Gate") is the ordinary word "gate"; en UI strings like "Confirmed" or "Gate proposal" are correct English but cannot literally contain the label form. **Referral to glossary-curator** (via PM): consider registering short-form en variants (`confirm/confirmed`, `gate`) or splitting verb/status senses for these two terms; either would collapse ~90% of the 274 entries. The `corp_code`/`MktSizing*` classes are key-vocabulary ("기업 공시 교차검증" rendered as "corporate filing cross-check") — candidate alias registrations rather than translation defects.
