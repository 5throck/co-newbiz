## Decisions
This is the first Stage 1 execution against the real roster rather than inline role-play (the three prior review meetings, in the ai_workspace root repo, predate co-newbiz's scaffold and used inline personas). Full decision log lives in each meeting file; the load-bearing ones:

- **O-2 resolved**: first target industry/region = shipbuilding-adjacent (crankshaft→marine-engine chain), Korea + EU (M1).
- **RAG thresholds made concrete**: Green/Amber/Red bands with actual IRR/DSCR numbers, confirmed as a priority-ordered rule (not a weighted average), hysteresis bands with real percentages and a 7-day hold measured against `run` timestamps (M3).
- **Correction to the design's chain-grading mental model**: grade is driven by *statistical* hop count, not total hop count — engineering hops (fixed physical coefficients) don't degrade the grade. This matters directly for the shipbuilding pilot, since crankshaft→engine is likely an engineering hop and engine→ship is the real statistical hop (M4).
- **M&A special-topic model linkage named concretely** per assumption_key, with an explicit evaluation order (antitrust/hr-integration cashflow effects first, customer-retention/incentive revenue-cost build second, certification transferability last since it can strip the entire premium) (M5).
- **Site scoring redesigned as two-stage**: hard pre-filter (infra minimums, partner-dependency confirmation) before the weighted score, not folded into the weights (M6).
- **Information protection made fully operational**: real-name mapping stays outside the system entirely; `comp_benchmark` minimum cohort size = 5; need-to-know enforcement is a static, testable repository-layer contract; MNPI enrollment is a four-step logged workflow (M7-1).
- **Certification decision tree fixed** for the pilot (ISO 9001 → classification approval), with transferability required to resolve by Pre-FS gate close, not DD, whenever it's load-bearing to the deal thesis (M7-2).
- **Correction to the design's default**: the concurrent-deal cap starts at **1**, not the design's stated "3" — that default assumed a reviewer baseline stronger than what's actually confirmed (O-16 is still open). Cap only moves to 3 once reviewer allocation reaches ≥1.0 FTE equivalent (M7-3).
- **No conflicts found** across all 12 meetings' outcomes at the M8 integrated review; one sequencing clarification made (M1's pre-filter and M6's weighted score are sequential, not competing checks).
- **O-1 resolved via design default**: `md_entity` seeded with placeholder entities pending a real list.
- **O-8 unchanged**: stays at its existing end-of-Stage-2 decision point.
- **Stage 1 formally closed** (D-M8-6) — Stage 2 entry criteria met per both the functional and technical domain leads.
