# Procedure: Group Investment Committee Review (Shared)

**Layer**: Procedure (mandatory — gate-blocking)

## Purpose

Group IC steps. Step 1 is the system-enforced Entity-IC-precondition check — this is the single most important structural rule in the two-tier approval chain (design §6.4): Group-level procedures are unreachable without a recorded Entity IC approval, and this procedure is where that check actually lives, not scattered across other procedures.

## Why Group IC Is Not Redundant With Entity IC

Per meeting M2: Entity IC asks "is this a good deal for us"; Group IC asks "is this a good deal for the group, and is it the best use of this capital right now." Steps 2–3 (portfolio fit, intra-group transaction compliance) are checks an entity-level reviewer has no visibility to perform. Step 4 (ranking) is usually the real decision — Group IC is a capital-allocation ranking across concurrent proposals more often than a single approve/reject.
