# Guideline: Expert Interview Conduct

**Layer**: Guideline (recommended — deviation requires a stated reason, not a blocked gate)

## When to Use
Commercial DD, technical DD, or any deal review requiring external expert input.

## Recommended Practice

1. **Compliance clearance before contact.** Confirm no NDA/MNPI conflict, and confirm the expert is not a current employee of a competitor without documented compliance sign-off.
2. **Triangulation is the target, not optional polish.** Any material claim should rest on at least 2-3 independent sources before it's relied on in an IC memo. A single-source claim gets flagged as such, not silently treated as confirmed.
3. **Structure the interview around disconfirming questions**, not just questions that would validate the deal thesis — an interview that only asks "why is this a good deal" produces confirmation bias, not diligence.
4. **Record source count and independence explicitly** in the interview writeup, so a reviewer can see triangulation strength at a glance rather than having to infer it from prose.

## Deviation

If fewer than 2 independent sources are available for a material claim, state that explicitly in the finding rather than presenting it with the same confidence as a triangulated claim. This is a recommended practice, not a Kill Criteria — but an untriangulated claim feeding a valuation assumption should be tagged `assumed` with the limitation noted in `rationale`, not `derived`.
