필수(mandatory) 항목은 활성 waiver 없이는 게이트를 통과할 수 없습니다. 권장 항목의 부재는 사유 진술로 충분합니다. 권위 원본은 저장소의 YAML입니다.

ID 항목 등급
ef-01 Jurisdiction and entity type selected with the tax/regulatory rationale documented and approved 필수
ef-02 Name availability cleared with the registry and preliminary trademark clearance completed 필수
ef-03 Share structure and parent/founder allocations fixed; initial directors and officers identified 필수
ef-04 Originating parent's internal approvals obtained — board/GM resolution authorizing formation, funding, and IP contribution 필수
ef-05 Charter/articles filed, registered address and agent appointed, registered capital paid in 필수
ef-06 Organizational documents executed — bylaws, incorporator action, board consents, director indemnification agreements 필수
ef-07 IP assignment into the entity complete — all pre-formation IP (parent background IP and originating team's work) assigned or licensed in writing; every employee and contractor signs a PIIA agreement 필수
ef-08 People transfer executed — new employment agreements + PIIAs signed; where staff come from the parent, a secondment or transfer agreement per applicable consent rules (Korea: business-transfer succession is judicial doctrine — 영업양도 — individual consent is the safe path; verify with counsel) 필수
ef-09 Intra-group agreements executed — intra-group services agreement, IP/brand license, supply or distribution agreements, cost-sharing/transfer-pricing policy documented 필수
ef-10 Statutory registrations completed — tax IDs, employer registrations, business licenses, sector permits; foreign-investment filings where cross-border 필수
ef-11 Banking, insurance, and accounting/audit arrangements opened; statutory books and registers established 필수
ef-12 Governance baseline set — first board minutes, delegated-authority matrix, compliance calendar, and a data room containing all of the above 필수
ef-13 Joint-origin only: board of 5-9 members with equal seats in a 50/50 and deadlock design (independent chair, casting vote, or rotating chair) agreed at formation 필수
ef-14 Joint-origin only: reserved-matters list requiring mutual consent — narrow, category-based, threshold-driven (strategy/scope, annual plan/budget, major capex/financing, CEO/CFO appointment, material related-party transactions, constitutional changes, ownership economics, dissolution) 필수
ef-15 Joint-origin only: deadlock ladder with binding deadlines — management reconciliation, board decision memo, shareholder principals, cooling-off/standstill, expert determination for defined factual/valuation issues, mediation, final remedy 필수
ef-16 Joint-origin only: exit clauses agreed — put/call with pre-agreed valuation formula, ROFR/ROFO and tag/drag rights, termination events, completion mechanics 필수
ef-17 Joint-origin only: one-voice management confirmed — direction to the venture only via board decisions and the CEO, with a written delegated-authority matrix (variance bands, emergency limits) 필수
ef-18 Joint-origin only: firewall/clean-team protocols established where the parents compete in adjacent markets 필수
ef-19 Korea note (only where a company split is the formation route): a split (물적분할/인적분할) requires a shareholders' special resolution and the creditor-protection procedure — individual notice to known creditors plus public notice with an objection window of at least one month (Commercial Act arts. 527-2/527-5). Verify against the current KLRI statute text before enforcement use. 권장
