pt-01 Capability gap defining the need for a partner is stated explicitly before sourcing starts 필수
pt-02 Compliance screening (sanctions, anti-corruption history) completed for every shortlisted partner candidate — not optional, since a partner's exposure transfers to us 필수
pt-03 Financial stability and capacity scores recorded per candidate with evidence references 필수
pt-04 Dependency risk assessed — including the risk the partner becomes a competitor after technology transfer 필수
pt-05 Where the business model has a hard partner dependency, partner-sourcing failure is modeled as a Kill Criteria candidate (deal termination), not a schedule delay (ADR-0031) 필수
pt-06 For JV structures: governance (board composition, veto matters), contribution valuation, exit clauses (put/call, drag/tag), and deadlock-resolution mechanism all documented before term-sheet negotiation 필수
pt-07 Technology contribution independently valued, not accepted at the contributing party's stated figure 권장
pt-08 Strategic complementarity assessed — what we bring plus what the partner brings jointly closes the named capability gap (pt-01), with the combination rationale written down 필수
pt-09 Partner technical capability evidenced against OUR next gate's requirement — test data, certifications, or demonstrated delivery, not marketing claims 필수
pt-10 Operational and cultural fit assessed — working style, decision speed, and the geographic/timezone operating reality of the collaboration 필수
pt-11 Alliance track record assessed, with references to at least two of the partner's other alliance partners as required evidence [rbd 2.4] 필수
pt-12 Organizational readiness on both sides — dedicated resources, a named executive sponsor, and decision authority delegated for the negotiation itself 필수
