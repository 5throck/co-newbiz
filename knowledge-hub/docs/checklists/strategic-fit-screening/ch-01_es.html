필수(mandatory) 항목은 활성 waiver 없이는 게이트를 통과할 수 없습니다. 권장 항목의 부재는 사유 진술로 충분합니다. 권위 원본은 저장소의 YAML입니다.

ID 항목 등급
sf-01 Strategic alignment cited by name — the opportunity advances at least one named objective of the current corporate/business-unit strategy, quoted or referenced by identifier (not 'aligns with strategy' in the abstract) 필수
sf-02 Portfolio/hunt-zone fit — the opportunity falls within a pre-declared investment thesis area (hunt zone), or carries an explicit exception approval from the portfolio owner recorded before the screen concludes 필수
sf-03 No disqualifying constraint — no known fatal safety, environmental, regulatory, sanctions, or reputational barrier 필수
sf-04 Strategic intent category declared (core-enhancement / adjacent-expansion / transformational bet) and that category's risk tolerance acknowledged by the sponsor 필수
sf-05 Named executive sponsor — one named executive owns the opportunity through the next gate and controls the seed budget 필수
sf-06 Preliminary market attractiveness scored and recorded, with the scorecard total meeting the advance threshold set for the declared intent category (desk research only at this gate) 필수
sf-07 Right-to-win hypothesis scored and recorded — at least one articulable advantage the company brings (asset, capability, channel, brand, data, license) that a generic entrant lacks 필수
sf-08 Synergy thesis scored and recorded — identifiable synergies with existing operations (channels, plants, technology, customer base) beyond standalone value 필수
sf-09 Capability-fit gap statement scored and recorded — required vs. current capabilities assessed, every material gap named with a plausible source (build / partner / buy) 필수
sf-10 Preliminary feasibility scored and recorded — no known insurmountable technical or operational obstacle, and rough time-to-market acceptable for the declared intent category 필수
sf-11 Reward-vs-risk scale scored and recorded — preliminary size of the prize is material relative to effort and risk, per the category's thresholds 필수
sf-12 Options discipline — the proposal is one of at least two routes to the stated objective, with a do-minimum/status-quo baseline recorded for comparison 필수
