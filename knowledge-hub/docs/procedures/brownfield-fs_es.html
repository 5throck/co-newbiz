# Gate Agent Skill Output Description
1 FS financial-analyst quality-of-earnings qoe_normalized_ebitda

Normalized EBITDA via QoE, feeding the valuation bridge. Scope is mandatory-light for internal-origin carve-outs (2026-08-22 design of record, Decision 5e): full third-party QoE scope drops, but cost-allocation integrity and transfer-pricing review remain in full — carve-out cost allocations and transfer prices are manipulable and survive the carve-out, so this is a scope reduction, never a skip. This step states the scope; the conditional per-origin scoping table lands with PR-5c's applicability engine (no per-origin QoE step variants here).
2 FS valuation-agent investment-feasibility-modeling full_valuation

NPV/IRR/Payback/DSCR model assembled from confirmed WACC/hurdle (Pre-FS output) and normalized EBITDA.
3 FS quant-methodologist monte-carlo-simulation distributional_valuation

Distributional valuation on the step-2 model (ADR-0052 §D.4): seeded Monte Carlo over the value drivers, integrating contingent liabilities as probability/triangular-distribution draws on every iteration — P50/P95 NPV and value-at-risk, never a point estimate with a narrative footnote.
4 FS legal-counsel investment-vehicle-structuring deal_structure_review

Four-axis review of the Pre-FS deal_structure_hypothesis — the shape axes (asset_or_stock, stake_type, instrument_mix, vehicle + commercialization_path) each reviewed against ADR-0045's four mandatory axes (tax structure, liability isolation, financing leverage, exit structure); legal-counsel owns the tax and liability axes. Completing the review sets four_axis_review_completed on the deal_structure_hypothesis record (column and table land via migration 0013; completion is a deliberate act, default 0 — referenced here, no DDL in this procedure). The deal_structure_review output records per-axis findings and any hypothesis revision for the IC package.
5 FS antitrust-agent antitrust-screening six_way_cross_check_started

Six-way M&A cross-check started here per meeting M5's fixed evaluation order — (1) antitrust + HR integration, (2) customer retention + incentive, (3) certification transferability last. Completion (all six landed) is a DD-exit requirement, not FS-exit.
6 FS quant-methodologist scenario-planning scenario_risk_assessment

FS risk assessment as discrete downside/base/upside scenarios (ADR-0052 §D.4) — including the standard +200bp rate-shock covenant test and any deal-specific structural scenario. Feeds the covenant-breach kill criterion and the DD risk workstreams.
7 FS market-intelligence-agent scenario-planning industry_structure_simulation

Industry-structure scenario simulation (ADR-0066 Phase C) — driver deltas (rate/fx/commodity/regulation from the run's drv.* assumptions) propagated through the value chain's elasticity and pass-through coefficients, persisting P05/P50/P95 demand/margin deltas per node. Owned by market-intelligence-agent per its six-axis charter; coefficient-less links surface as UNASSESSED nodes, never zero impact.
7.5 FS financial-analyst financial-statement-analysis fs_canonical_kpi_baseline

Canonical-model KPI baseline (profitability, growth, leverage, cash flow) feeding QoE and feasibility inputs (graph-pilot wiring, backfill-report §4.1).
7.6 FS financial-analyst peer-benchmarking peer_benchmark_set

Comparable-company benchmark of target KPIs — cross-sectional counterpart to historical trend analysis (graph-pilot wiring).
7.7 FS quant-methodologist proxy-industry-chain proxy_demand_chain

Proxy-chain demand estimation where direct market data is absent; chain reliability graded A/B/C on statistical hops only (graph-pilot wiring).
7.8 FS quant-methodologist unit-conversion-calibration unit_conversion_calibration

OLS calibration of a proxy-chain hop with lead-lag search and 95% confidence interval (graph-pilot wiring).
7.9 FS financial-analyst value-chain-stage-financials chain_stage_financial_rollup

Per-value-chain-node 5-year KPI rollup (ADR-0066) — revenue growth, margin, and ROIC per chain stage assembled from DART company data into fact_industry, with member-company list and aggregation method. The historical baseline the step-7 structure simulation is sanity-checked against.
7.95 FS quant-methodologist value-chain-driver-identification chain_node_sensitivity_set

Per-stage driver sensitivities derived by correlation/lead-lag estimation of each node's financial series against registered driver series, written to chain_node_sensitivity with fit quality and confidence grade — replacing unexamined manual entries as the step-7 simulation's sensitivity inputs.
8 FS process-governance-manager stage-gate-governance gate_pass_record

Confirm FS deliverables complete; open DD.
