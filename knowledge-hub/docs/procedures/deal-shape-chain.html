단계

# Gate Agent Skill Output Description
1 P0 partnership-agent partner-sourcing-strategy partner_sourcing_funnel

Need & funnel entry [rbd 4.1, 4.2]: a named technology-roadmap gap justifies an external search before any candidate work starts — pt-01 of checklists/partnership-checklist.yaml (capability gap stated before sourcing) is the entry condition. The scouting funnel assigns each candidate a recommended deal shape and route; funnel discipline follows ADR-0031 (a hard partner dependency that fails to source is a kill candidate, never a schedule delay — pt-05).
2 P1 partnership-agent partner-sourcing-strategy partner_fit_screen_result

Strategic fit & partner screen — knock-out + scorecard on the PARTNERSHIP, not the market [rbd 2.2], reusing the knock-out+scorecard method of strategic-fit-screening (PR-10) with partner-facing criteria: strategic complementarity, technical capability against our next gate, operational/cultural fit, financial health, alliance track record, readiness (pt-02..pt-04, pt-08..pt-12 of checklists/partnership-checklist.yaml v1.1.0). Scorecard weights are ILLUSTRATIVE until the thresholds meeting (ADR-0052 open question d). Every rejection records written feedback (screen decision vocabulary Go/Kill/Hold/Recycle).
3 P2 partnership-agent partner-due-diligence partner_dd_report

Partner due diligence — the seven workstreams [rbd 2.3, 2.6]: legal/ title, technical (TRL/MRL evidence against OUR next gate), collaboration capability, financial, commercial-conflict, cultural, exit/ reversibility — producing the standard report with a deal-shape recommendation. Referencing at least two of the partner's other alliance partners is a required evidence item [rbd 2.4]. Red findings here feed the kill criteria below.
4 P3 legal-counsel jv-structuring deal_shape_decision

Deal-shape selection on the five criteria (design §A.3: control need, IP position, capital at risk, exit optionality, time-to-market) with the P2 report's recommendation as input; JV-shaped outcomes get the full jv-structuring method [rbd 3.4]. The full-M&A outcome ROUTES OUT to the brownfield chain unchanged (design §A.2 last row — this chain stops there). The decision writes the classification axes per ADR-0047 §A.5: license-in/co-development typically greenfield+external, minority greenfield+hybrid, JV greenfield+joint, M&A brownfield+external — the chain never invents a fifth origin value.
5 P4 legal-counsel investment-vehicle-structuring presigning_shape_check

Contracting & execution: the pre-signing gate is keyed to the selected shape's checklist in checklists/deal-shape-presigning-checklist.yaml (license-in / co-development / minority / JV — design §A.2), with structure review reusing investment-vehicle-structuring (PR-10). For entity-bearing shapes, formation ROUTES INTO entity-establishment-execution (this chain owns the decision to choose the shape, not the formation itself — design §A.2 JV row); JV formation mechanics ride the entity-formation JV addendum ef-13..17.
6 P5 partnership-agent project-review alliance_post_launch_review

Launch & post-launch review [rbd 2.1 phase 6-7]: kickoff plan, then a dated review of the alliance against its SELECTION RATIONALE (the P3 decision, not a generic health check). Owned by partnership-agent (design open decision 4, PM decision 2026-08-23); feeds JV lifecycle monitoring (design §C) for JV-shaped outcomes or portfolio monitoring (design §E) for minority stakes — both PR-12-sequenced.
