# Gate Agent Skill Output Description
1 DD commercial-dd-agent commercial-due-diligence dd_finding

Customer/channel/pricing-power checks per checklists/commercial-dd-checklist.yaml.
2 DD technical-dd-agent technical-due-diligence dd_finding

Site visit, remaining equipment life, TRL, EHS per checklists/technical-dd-checklist.yaml.
3 DD technical-dd-agent technical-due-diligence dd_finding

R&BD deals only (technology_track: rbd on the 3-axis classification — technology/IP acquisition, license-in, or internal-venture spin-off carve-out; the case_subtype field this step previously cited was retired by ADR-0047): run checklists/rbd-technology-checklist.yaml in addition to step 2 — covers core-technology TRL (distinct from production-process TRL), government co-funding conditions, IP/freedom-to-operate diligence, the build/license/spin-off commercialization-path decision, and continuing parent-entity relationship terms where the target originated as an internal venture. See docs/pilots/pilot-011-posco-amberoad.md. Not applicable (skip) for non-R&BD deals.
4 DD market-intelligence-agent competitive-intelligence dd_finding

Market DD per checklists/market-dd-checklist.yaml (mdd-01..) — TAM/SAM triangulation, competitive set, moat, customer concentration, churn, 80/20 revenue vs. thesis. Thesis-by-thesis validation verdicts (validated/partially/invalidated) feed the valuation bridge via retention-adjusted revenue impact and bid-adjustment recommendations. Runs alongside, not inside, commercial DD (step 1): step 1 tests the TARGET's commercial position; this step tests the MARKET the target sits in (ADR-0051).
5 DD legal-counsel legal-due-diligence dd_finding

Legal DD per checklists/legal-dd-checklist.yaml (ldd-01..) — corporate standing/cap table, CoC/anti-assignment contract scan, litigation, IP title chain, employment/legal exposure, environmental/real-estate/ data-protection/anti-bribery/tax. Output is a RAG findings register: red = deal-structural/remediation, amber = warranty/indemnity, green = acceptable. Red/amber findings map to SPA protections (specific indemnities, escrow sizing, CPs) and valuation adjustments through the dd-to-valuation-bridge. Distinct from the certification-transferability step (step 11) and from screening-level chk-legal-regulatory — legal basis for transferability lives there, this step supplies the evidence.
6 DD antitrust-agent antitrust-screening dd_finding

HHI/HHI-delta finalized, multi-jurisdiction filing confirmed, gun-jumping check — checklists/ma-special-topics-checklist.yaml ma-01/02/03. Covers common-ownership/common-control combinations (a sponsor already controlling one competitor acquiring another), not only direct mergers of the target entities — docs/pilots/pilot-010-affinity-lotte-rental.md is a real prohibited deal of exactly this shape, the pilot set's first outright-block outcome. Real-case pattern reference: docs/pilots/pilot-001-hd-hyundai-stx-heavy-industries.md (horizontal, conditional approval) vs. docs/pilots/pilot-002-hanwha-impact-hsd-engine.md (vertical, unconditional approval) — the same step must correctly discriminate between these risk shapes, not apply one template.
7 DD hr-integration-agent management-incentive-review dd_finding

Executive CoC-trigger compensation and compensation-harmonization-analysis — checklists/ma-special-topics-checklist.yaml ma-04 through ma-06.
8 DD customer-retention-agent customer-attrition-risk dd_finding

Change-of-control clause scan across customer AND supplier contracts (checklist ma-07/08, widened per docs/pilots/pilot-002-hanwha-impact-hsd-engine.md finding F-2 — a retained seller-side supply relationship is the same risk category as a customer contract).
9 DD incentive-agent incentive-clawback-assessment dd_finding

Clawback exposure on any contemplated relocation/restructuring — checklist ma-09.
10 DD valuation-agent debt-and-contingent-liability-simulation dd_finding

Contingent liabilities and covenant-breach scenario — checklist ma-10/11.
11 DD legal-counsel certification-transferability dd_finding

Certification transferability confirmed LAST among the validators per meeting M5's fixed evaluation order (checklist ma-12) — a non-transferable certification can strip the entire acquisition premium and must not be computed against a valuation that already assumed it. The transferability judgment is legal-counsel's call (design of record 2026-08-22 §5 PR-3, meeting legal-review findings #1/#6); certification-agent provides the requirement/roadmap input.
12 DD valuation-agent financing-plan-review financing_readiness_check

Financing evidence pack per checklists/financing-plan-checklist.yaml (fin-01..) — cash/debt/equity mix with sources-and-uses, executed commitment papers or documented internal-funds approval path, covenant headroom, funds-flow memorandum draft, FX plan, escrow sizing, reverse-termination-fee sizing where buyer retains funding risk (4-7% EV benchmark ILLUSTRATIVE until the thresholds meeting — ADR-0051 open question (c)). Certainty of funds is evidenced here so the IC package carries a financing_readiness_check, not a financing contingency.
13 DD hr-integration-agent org-readiness-assessment dd_finding

HR DD per checklists/hr-dd-checklist.yaml (hdd-01..) — key-person map with retention actions, stay-bonus budget (12-24 month benchmark ILLUSTRATIVE — ADR-0051 open question (c)), org/leadership bench, CBA successorship and pension exposure, employment-transfer regime per jurisdiction, culture distance, day-1 and first-100-days plan. Compensation review stays in the validator step (step 7); this step is the wider organizational and labor-relations picture.
14 DD compliance-officer compliance-monitoring six_validator_completeness_check

Six-validator completeness is recorded as THREE numbers — declared 6 / applicable N / completed M (ADR-0048 §4; the durable record is written by db/repositories/compliance.repository.ts). All six M&A validators (steps 6-11, mapped to antitrust/HR/customer/incentive/contingent- liability/certification — certification LAST, unchanged relative order) are DECLARED for every project. A validator the applicability registry does not apply to the live classification (ADR-0048 — the only skip family is greenfield+internal, contingent-liability regained when technology_track=rbd) is covered-with-reason: a compliance_check row result='not_applicable' carrying the rendered applies_when sentence plus the classification and approver trace — NEVER 'pass'. The no-waiver halt (kill criterion below) fires when an APPLICABLE validator is missing; an applicable validator with no coverage row fails this step regardless of anything else. This is a completeness check with NO WAIVER PATH (design §8.9-1(f), docs/governance-operations.md §2) — not a judgment call. The DD cross-validation (Decisions 5f/5g) runs alongside: classification- independent DD evidence contradicting the classification blocks DD exit only when the classification's provenance is weak. The four DD-expansion steps (4 market, 5 legal, 12 financing, 13 HR) are OUTSIDE the no-waiver core: mandatory but with the standard waiver path (ADR-0051; whether legal DD joins the core is an open question for the thresholds meeting, not decided here).
15 DD process-governance-manager stage-gate-governance gate_pass_record

Confirm DD deliverables complete; open Entity IC.
