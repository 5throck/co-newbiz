# 게이트 에이전트 스킬 산출물 설명
1 post_close legal-counsel jv-structuring jv_governance_review

Operate [pmi-jv B.1]: board-approved JV governance framework review — only 45% of JVs have one (ILLUSTRATIVE; cited as the practice gap this step closes). Directors-only board attendance; quarterly board cadence plus one annual strategy off-site; reserved-matters map (board vs shareholder) with conflict-prone decisions moved to shareholders; conflict-of-interest protocol covering the three JV conflict types (business opportunity, self-dealing, information disclosure) with a deliberately-chosen fiduciary-duty approach; arm's-length pricing for parent-JV commercial relationships. Formation wrote these terms (deal-shape-chain P3/P4 + entity-formation JV addendum ef-14); this step reviews governance as it actually operates.
2 post_close partnership-agent project-review jv_health_scorecard

Monitor [pmi-jv B.2]: per-JV health scorecard across seven dimensions — performance, people, partner alignment, decision health, milestones & funding, governance health, value-distribution symmetry. Scorecard weights and cadence are a thresholds-meeting item (ADR-0052 open question b); independent external governance review every 3 years (ILLUSTRATIVE cadence). Outputs persist via db/repositories/venture-scorecard.repository.ts recordVentureScorecardCycle with source_procedure 'jv-lifecycle-governance' (ADR-0059, migration 0024): the seven dimensions map into the financial/strategic sections, and measure_key preserves dimension identity.
3 post_close group-review-agent portfolio-fit-assessment jv_strategic_asset_review

Annual strategic asset review per JV [pmi-jv B.2] carrying the parent-side disciplines of B.6: designated executive accountability for JVs (only 38% of companies have it, ILLUSTRATIVE), no "corporate adhocracy", a JV portfolio gameboard by venture type × lifecycle phase, post-close lookbacks feeding standards. Feeds the Section E portfolio rollup (portfolio-venture-monitoring step 1).
4 post_close legal-counsel jv-structuring jv_restructuring_decision

Restructure [pmi-jv B.3, B.4]: formal triggers tied to metrics, not crisis-waiting — the baseline expectation that at least half of JVs fail on >=1 owner objective and the median 39-month restructuring lag vs comparable wholly-owned businesses (both ILLUSTRATIVE) are the reason triggers must be metric-tied. Restructuring menu: scope, ownership, governance, economics, operating model, leadership. Deadlock ladder with binding time-boxes — functional managers 15-30d -> senior executives 30-60d -> CEOs -> institutional mediation (day ranges ILLUSTRATIVE). Pre-agreed evolution paths in the JV agreement ("inability to evolve" is a named failure cause).
5 post_close legal-counsel jv-structuring jv_exit_execution

Exit — designed at formation, executed here [pmi-jv B.5]: the five-step exit-design practice (strategic end-game; trigger selection from 16 categories, typically 4-8 adopted (ILLUSTRATIVE); mechanisms put/call/buy-sell with ROFR/tag-drag protections; valuation including the two-appraisers-then-third process; post-exit transition across six areas — IP licensing, people/secondees, services, supply & purchase, non-competes, debt/guarantees). Exit paths: sale to partner (65% of endings, ILLUSTRATIVE), third-party sale, IPO, unwind. Consumes the formation-time exit clauses the entity-formation JV addendum (ef-16) wrote — no duplication: this step exercises the mechanics formation created. Decision to exercise routes through Group IC (investment decisions are human, boundary #2).
