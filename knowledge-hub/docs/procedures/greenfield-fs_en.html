# Gate Agent Skill Output Description
1 FS financial-analyst growth-projection-modeling growth_projection

Anchored growth projection (design §4-4) — every derived figure carries an anchor_ref. Base-rate check: outside the top decile of historical/peer distribution requires risk-officer sign-off, not a silent pass.
2 FS greenfield-agent site-infrastructure-assessment capex_schedule_with_permit_critical_path

Full capex schedule sequenced against the permit DAG's critical path (permit-pathway-mapping) — permit lead time is a direct IRR input, not a separate footnote.
3 FS valuation-agent investment-feasibility-modeling valuation_model

NPV/IRR/Payback/DSCR model assembled from confirmed WACC/hurdle (Pre-FS output) and the anchored growth projection.
4 FS quant-methodologist monte-carlo-simulation distributional_valuation

Distributional valuation on the step-3 model (ADR-0052 §D.4): seeded Monte Carlo over the value drivers — P50/P95 NPV and value-at-risk, never a point estimate with a narrative footnote. The proxy-chain confidence problem (a two-hop proxy estimate cannot reach Green, boundary #4) is exactly where distributional valuation earns its keep: chain reliability enters as the dispersion of the demand driver, and step 1's anchored growth projection is the input contract.
5 FS quant-methodologist scenario-planning scenario_risk_assessment

FS risk assessment as discrete downside/base/upside scenarios (ADR-0052 §D.4) — including the rate-shock covenant test where project financing covenants bind. Feeds the covenant-breach kill criterion and the DD risk workstreams.
6 FS market-intelligence-agent scenario-planning industry_structure_simulation

Industry-structure scenario simulation (ADR-0066 Phase C) — driver deltas (rate/fx/commodity/regulation from the run's drv.* assumptions) propagated through the value chain's elasticity and pass-through coefficients, persisting P05/P50/P95 demand/margin deltas per node. Owned by market-intelligence-agent per its six-axis charter; coefficient-less links surface as UNASSESSED nodes, never zero impact.
6.5 FS financial-analyst historical-financial-analysis historical_financial_base_rate

Multi-year trend/volatility/cyclicality base-rate distribution that growth-projection-modeling anchors against (graph-pilot wiring, backfill-report §4.1).
6.6 FS financial-analyst peer-benchmarking peer_benchmark_set

Comparable-company KPI benchmark — cross-sectional counterpart to the historical base rate (graph-pilot wiring).
6.7 FS quant-methodologist proxy-industry-chain proxy_demand_chain

Proxy-chain demand estimation where no direct market data exists (graph-pilot wiring).
6.8 FS quant-methodologist unit-conversion-calibration unit_conversion_calibration

Per-hop statistical calibration of the proxy chain (graph-pilot wiring).
6.9 FS financial-analyst value-chain-stage-financials chain_stage_financial_rollup

Per-value-chain-node 5-year KPI rollup (ADR-0066) — revenue growth, margin, and ROIC per chain stage assembled from DART company data into fact_industry, with member-company list and aggregation method. The historical baseline the step-6 structure simulation is sanity-checked against.
6.95 FS quant-methodologist value-chain-driver-identification chain_node_sensitivity_set

Per-stage driver sensitivities derived by correlation/lead-lag estimation of each node's financial series against registered driver series, written to chain_node_sensitivity with fit quality and confidence grade — replacing unexamined manual entries as the step-6 simulation's sensitivity inputs.
7 FS process-governance-manager stage-gate-governance gate_pass_record

Confirm FS deliverables complete; open DD.
