# Gate Agent Skill Output Description
1 Target Screening brownfield-agent strategic-fit-screening target_screening_result

Run the knock-out screens per candidate against checklists/target-screening-checklist.yaml (chk-target-screening, ADR-0081): t-01 ownership/availability, t-02 regulatory (incl. concentration pre-screen), t-03 geographic fit, t-04 structural market contraction+commoditization, t-05 capability relevance vs the S1/S3 required-capability list. Every knock-out failure is recorded with a written exclusion reason (Exclusion Reasons); the rated scorecard (t-06..t-09) feeds the Target Ranking. Desk research only — no commissioned analysis before the target_screening gate.
2 Target Screening antitrust-agent antitrust-screening target_regulatory_screen

Per-candidate concentration/regulatory pre-screen for candidates with external market presence — early filter only; the full six-way cross-check runs at DD. Reuses the fact-gated rule of shared-brownfield-screening step 3 (fail-closed on unassessed facts).
3 Target Screening brownfield-agent peer-benchmarking target_ranking

Rank the surviving candidates on the rated scorecard (strategic value, capability complementarity, financial attractiveness, integration/ partnership complexity) calibrated against peer benchmarks. Produce the Target Ranking/Score with per-criterion evidence and propose the Short List cut — the cut line itself is confirmed at the target_screening gate.
4 Target Screening process-governance-manager stage-gate-governance gate_pass_record

Confirm the Target Screening gate deliverables — Short List exists, every Long List candidate has a ranking or exclusion reason, and all Short List candidates passed the knock-out screens — then open S8 (사업성 분석) for the Short List targets.
